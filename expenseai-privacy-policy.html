Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2025

Welcome to Jotto.Shop ExpenseAI ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share data when you use our expense-tracking application (the "Service"), including features such as uploading receipt images, synchronizing bank or PDF statements, and managing income and expenses.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Service, we collect the following categories of information:

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information — name, email address, password, and authentication tokens.
  • Financial Data — manual entries of expenses or income, budgets, and tags you create.
  • Uploads — photos of receipts, PDFs, and other files you choose to submit.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data — interactions with the app, feature usage statistics, and diagnostics.
  • Device and Log Data — IP address, browser type, operating system, and timestamps.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Bank Integrations — transaction data and statement details you authorize us to access via secure APIs or Open Banking feeds. We do not retrieve or store bank account numbers, card numbers, routing numbers, or any other sensitive account identifiers; only statements and individual transaction records are retained. All connections are strictly read-only; Jotto.Shop ExpenseAI cannot initiate payments, transfers, or modifications to your bank accounts.
  • Cloud Storage Providers — files you connect or import.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service;
  • Process and classify receipts via Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and Machine Learning;
  • Sync, categorize, and reconcile bank transactions and PDF statements;
  • Generate insights, trends, and analytics to help you understand spending habits;
  • Authenticate users, prevent fraud, and ensure account security;
  • Respond to user requests, support tickets, and feedback;
  • Improve and personalize the Service, including testing new features;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. AI & OCR Processing

When you upload an image or PDF, we process it using optical character recognition and proprietary AI models hosted in secure cloud environments. The extracted text (e.g., merchant name, date, amount) is stored in our database to enable categorization and search. Uploaded files may be temporarily cached during processing but are encrypted at rest once stored.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data:

  • With service providers who help us operate the Service (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics, OCR), bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • When required by law or to respond to valid legal requests;
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Jotto.Shop ExpenseAI, our users, or the public;
  • With your explicit consent or at your direction.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to operate the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this Policy.

  • Active accounts. Data remains accessible while your Jotto.Shop ExpenseAI account is active so that you can view historical spending, budgets, and documents.
  • Immediate deletion on request. You may permanently erase all personal data at any time by selecting Delete Account (or Clean My Data) in the app settings. Once confirmed, all receipts, statements, transactions, budgets, and profile details are irreversibly purged from our primary databases immediately.
  • Back-ups & logs. System back-ups containing your data are automatically overwritten within 24 hours. Minimal audit logs may be retained for up to 30 days for security and anti-fraud purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymized.
  • Legal obligations. If we are legally required to keep certain records (e.g., tax or accounting regulations), we will retain only the information mandated by law and delete it as soon as permitted.

6. International Data Transfers

If you access the Service from outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be transferred, stored, and processed in other countries. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure that your data remains protected in accordance with this Policy.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Access & Correction — You can access and update most of your personal data directly in the app.
  • Deletion — You may delete your account and data at any time from the settings page.
  • Opt-out — You can opt out of marketing notifications and analytics tracking through the app settings.
  • Data Portability — Request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.

8. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard safeguards such as TLS in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, least-privilege access controls, and regular penetration testing. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notice. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest revision.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@expenseai.io.
